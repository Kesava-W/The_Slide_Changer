Synopsis

At a rooftop restaurant in Hyderabad, Dhriti asks her friends a question that sounds like a game. Would you rather marry a man who earns less than you but is plainly capable, or one who earns more than he has any right to? Nobody gives the answer she is hoping for.

Dhriti earns nine lakhs a year as a junior HR executive. Sahishnu, whose ambitious healthcare project collapsed when its funding did, earns three as a data-entry operator. She has fallen for him before they have ever met, and she is sure that if he learns her salary, he will say no before the first date. So she bends the truth, and drafts a reluctant mutual friend to carry it.

A lie that small was never going to stay small. Told out of order, this is a story about money, merit and what a family will and will not forgive - and about the woman who decided, quietly, exactly what she was willing to risk.

Main Characters

Dhriti

A junior HR executive in Hyderabad, still living with her parents. Warm, stubborn and far more strategic than she lets on, she has a habit of asking the question everyone else would rather avoid.

Sahishnu

A hands-on learner who never blamed the system for the exams he could not crack. He has built something real once and watched it fall apart, and he is not sure he can afford to believe in himself again.

Manish

Dhriti's friend and Sahishnu's family friend, and so the one person standing between them. He has a secret of his own, which turns out to be the easiest thing in the world to use against him.

Vijay

Sahishnu's closest friend, a young doctor and the man whose idea started the project. Steady, practical, and better at hoping than he admits.

Genres & Themes

Genres: Contemporary Romance, Relationship Drama, Slice of Life

Themes: Love versus Expectation, Money and Merit, Family Pressure, Small Lies, Choosing Your Own Life
